Two of Syntara’s rules point different ways, a past decision doesn’t fit what you’re seeing, or two products need opposite things. Raise it here: answer a few questions, and GitHub opens with everything filled in.

<Callout tone="info" title="What happens next">
  The design lead sorts it, decides within the [review times](/docs/governance#how-a-change-gets-in), and writes the reason on the issue. Every decided conflict ends in a decision record that names who decided. How each kind is settled is on [Governance](/docs/governance#when-things-conflict).
</Callout>

## Before you raise it

- **Two rules clash?** The priority order settles it, without a debate: **accessibility over not breaking consumers over brand wishes over speed.**
- **Disagree with a past decision?** It’s reopened only with new information — a measurement, a bug, or a need nobody knew about. Check the [decision records](/docs/governance#decision-records) first.
- **Two products want opposite things?** Look for a prop, a variant or a brand input that serves both. A brand is data, not a fork.
